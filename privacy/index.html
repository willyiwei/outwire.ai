POLICY / PRIVACY

Privacy,
plainly.

Outwire collects as little information as practical. This page explains what stays on your device, what third-party services receive, and the choices available to you.

Effective July 17, 2026

At a glance

  • Checklist answers remain in your browser.
  • Outwire does not attach checklist answers to your email.
  • Outwire does not sell personal information.
  • No advertising trackers or behavioral analytics are currently used.
01

Checklist data stays on your device

The DEF CON 34 AI Application Security Checklist stores your review statuses and optional application-profile answers in your browser’s local storage. This lets your progress survive a refresh without creating an Outwire account.

Outwire does not receive this checklist data. It remains on the device and browser where you completed the checklist until you reset it, clear browser data, or use browser controls that remove local storage.

Copying or printing results happens on your device. Outwire does not receive a copy.

02

Email subscriptions are handled by Substack

The checklist page includes an official Substack subscription form. When you interact with that embedded form, your email address and subscription activity are provided directly to Substack and are governed by Substack’s terms and privacy policy.

Checklist answers are not sent to Substack or attached to an email address. You can use the entire checklist without subscribing.

You can unsubscribe using the link in any Substack email or manage your subscription through Substack.

03

Normal website delivery data

Like most websites, Outwire’s hosting and network providers may process basic technical information needed to deliver and protect the site. This can include an IP address, browser and device type, requested page, time of request, and diagnostic or security events.

Outwire does not currently use advertising trackers, cross-site behavioral tracking, device fingerprinting, or a separate analytics product.

04

Third-party services

Substack provides the embedded email subscription form and newsletter service.

Google Fonts provides site typography. Your browser may connect to Google to retrieve font files and, as part of that request, transmit standard connection information such as your IP address.

Website hosting and content delivery providers deliver the site and may maintain limited operational or security logs.

Links to YouTube, LinkedIn, X, GitHub, and other sites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Outwire does not control those external sites.

05

Your choices

  • Use the checklist without providing an email address.
  • Select Reset progress to delete checklist review statuses from local storage.
  • Select Clear answers to delete optional application-profile answers.
  • Clear site data using your browser settings.
  • Block third-party content or cookies using browser privacy controls, although this may prevent the Substack form or web fonts from loading.
  • Unsubscribe from Outwire emails through Substack at any time.

Children’s privacy

Outwire is intended for AI builders and security professionals. It is not directed to children under 13, and Outwire does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site, service providers, or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when material updates are published.
